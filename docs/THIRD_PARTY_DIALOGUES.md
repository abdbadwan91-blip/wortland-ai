# Dialogue corpus attribution

WortLand AI uses/adapts task-oriented dialogue material from **BConTrasT**, created for the WMT20 Chat Translation Task by Unbabel and collaborators.

Source: https://github.com/Unbabel/BConTrasT

Dataset license: **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)**.

Adaptations for WortLand AI can include CEFR simplification, pedagogical annotations, topic mapping, answer distractors, translations, speaker labels, and game metadata. Adapted corpus material must retain attribution and be distributed under the applicable ShareAlike terms.

Citation:
M. Amin Farajian, António V. Lopes, André F. T. Martins, Sameen Maruf, Gholamreza Haffari (2020), *Findings of the WMT 2020 Shared Task on Chat Translation*.
